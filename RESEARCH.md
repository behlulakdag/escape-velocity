## Lessons
- Make the theme the VERB, not the wallpaper. Fun + on-theme beats either alone.
- Movement/input feel is the biggest Fun lever. Avoid multi-button touch controls.
- Coherent look (bold outlines, good lighting, not too dark). Procedural art can score high.
- Celebrate success: combos, pitch-rising SFX, finisher moments, upgrade picks.
- First 10 seconds teach the game. Always have music + SFX. Instant restart.

## One-verb portrait references (generic)
Stack (tap to drop), Knife Hit (tap to throw), Helix Jump (drag to rotate world), Flappy (tap impulse),
Crossy Road (tap hop), Color Switch, slingshot (pull & release), Ball Blast (drag + auto-fire),
Rise Up (drag shield), Archero (stop to shoot).
Shared: same-frame response, readable fair failure, escalating reward, rising speed, <1s retry.
